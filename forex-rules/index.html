The Three Checks

You don't need a new strategy. You need three checks and somewhere to write them down. This is the free one-page version — keep it open, or screenshot it.

1 — The Gate

Five questions, before you click.

  1. Is this trade in my plan?
  2. Is the stop set?
  3. Is it sized to my risk?
  4. Am I clear of news?
  5. Am I chasing a loss?

One “no” answer is a no-go. Not a smaller size — a no-go.

2 — The Room

How much room you actually have left today.

A trade can pass all five questions above and still end your challenge, because passing the Gate says nothing about what the rest of the day has already cost you. So work out the room before you size.

Say your daily limit is $500. You're down $200 on closed trades and $150 on open ones. If your firm counts open trades toward the limit — most do — your real remaining room is $147, not $300. Size from $147.

On gold, stops slip. Leave room for it rather than sizing to the exact number.

3 — The Receipt

The one nobody likes, and the only one that answers “am I up?”

  • Every fee, every challenge, every reset — spent.
  • Every payout actually received — received.
  • Received minus spent. That's the number.

Passing a challenge feels like winning. The Receipt is what tells you whether you are.
